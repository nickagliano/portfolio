Nick Agliano

Principal Software Engineer, grid and utility software

I've spent six years building grid software alongside power systems engineers and utility customers. Interconnection studies, hosting capacity, outage planning, and generator monitoring.

In my experience, choosing between Postgres and ClickHouse is easy. Running a fleet of on-prem servers is hard work, but it isn't a hard problem. The truly hard problems are design and product: figuring out what to build, and making it simple.

My favorite moment is when a hard problem gets a solution so simple that people say, "Well, that's obvious."

I love open-source software, open standards, and grassroots communities. I contribute to Rust projects like Ratatui, and I've written about the CIM, the open standard for describing the power grid.
